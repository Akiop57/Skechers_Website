Comfort you can feel in the first step

Four technologies sit behind most of our lineup. Here is what each one does for your feet.

🦶

Hands Free Slip-ins

A molded heel pillow holds your heel in place, so you step in without bending down or touching the shoe.

🌬️

Air-Cooled Memory Foam

A soft insole that shapes to your foot and vents heat, so long days stay cool.

⛰️

Arch Fit

A podiatrist-designed insole with firm arch support for people who are on their feet all day.

☁️

Max Cushioning

A thick, bouncy midsole that absorbs impact on hard floors and pavement.
